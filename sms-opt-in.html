SMS Opt-In Workflow

Last updated: July 9, 2026

This page documents the SMS opt-in workflow for Connor Knabe's personal RuneLite/OSRS notifier side project. The notifier is used only for low-volume SMS alerts sent to the owner's own phone number. It is not a public signup service and does not enroll third-party customers, leads, subscribers, or public users.

SMS notifications are optional. The notifier can be used without opting in to SMS messages. Providing a phone number and checking the SMS consent box are not required to use the notifier, and consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of using the service or completing any action.

Who Receives Messages

The only intended SMS recipient is the project owner. No third-party end users are enrolled. Messages are sent only if the owner voluntarily configures and enables SMS notifications for the owner's own phone number.

Opt-In Path

  1. The owner may use the notifier without SMS notifications.
  2. If the owner wants SMS notifications, the owner may optionally enter their mobile phone number.
  3. The owner reviews the SMS consent disclosure shown below.
  4. The owner may optionally check the SMS consent checkbox.
  5. The owner may submit the optional SMS opt-in request.
  6. The owner may also choose “Continue Without SMS Notifications” and still use the notifier without SMS messages.
  7. SMS alerts are sent only if the owner voluntarily opts in to SMS notifications.
