SMS Opt-In Workflow

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This page documents the SMS opt-in workflow for Connor Knabe's personal RuneLite/OSRS notifier side project. The notifier is used only for low-volume SMS alerts sent to the owner's own phone number. It is not a public signup service and does not enroll third-party customers, leads, subscribers, or public users.

Who Receives Messages

The only intended SMS recipient is the project owner. No third-party end users are enrolled. Messages are sent only after the owner manually configures and enables SMS notifications for the owner's own phone number.

Opt-In Path

  1. The owner opens the notifier application's SMS notification settings.
  2. The owner enters their own mobile phone number.
  3. The owner verifies that the number belongs to them.
  4. The owner reviews the SMS consent disclosure shown below.
  5. The owner actively enables SMS notifications by checking the consent checkbox.
  6. The notifier sends SMS alerts only after SMS notifications are enabled.

SMS Consent Disclosure Shown During Opt-In

Enable SMS Notifications

By checking this box, I agree to receive SMS notifications from Connor Knabe RuneLite Notifier at the phone number I provided. Messages may include RuneLite/OSRS gameplay notifier alerts, including low Hitpoints, low Prayer, XP rate updates, valuable loot drops, idle status, and other configured notifier events. Message frequency varies based on gameplay activity and notifier settings. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

I understand that SMS consent is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. I can review the Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions at the links below.

Opt-Out and Help

The recipient may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. The recipient may reply HELP for help. SMS notifications may also be disabled in the notifier application's settings.
