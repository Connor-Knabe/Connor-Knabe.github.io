Terms and Conditions

Last updated: June 27, 2026

These Terms and Conditions apply to Connor Knabe's personal RuneLite/OSRS notifier side project (the "Notifier"). The Notifier sends low-volume SMS notifications related to the owner's own Old School RuneScape gameplay and RuneLite client alerts.

Use of SMS Notifications

The Notifier may send SMS alerts about configured gameplay events, including low Hitpoints, low Prayer, XP rate updates, valuable loot drops, idle status, or other RuneLite/OSRS notifier events selected by the owner.

Consent

The only intended SMS recipient is the owner of the Notifier. Consent is provided when the owner manually configures and verifies their own phone number and enables SMS notifications.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on gameplay activity and notifier settings.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out

The recipient may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. SMS notifications may also be disabled in the notifier application's settings.

No Marketing Messages

The Notifier does not send promotional, marketing, sales, lead generation, or third-party messages.
