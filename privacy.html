Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Connor Knabe's personal RuneLite/OSRS notifier side project (the "Notifier"). The Notifier is used for low-volume SMS alerts related to the owner's own Old School RuneScape gameplay and RuneLite client notifications.

Purpose of SMS Messages

SMS messages are used only to send gameplay-related notifier alerts to the owner's own phone number. Alerts may include low Hitpoints, low Prayer, XP rate updates, valuable loot drops, idle status, or other configured RuneLite/OSRS notifier events.

Information Collected

The Notifier may store the owner's phone number and basic notification settings needed to send SMS alerts. The Notifier is not intended for use by third-party customers, subscribers, leads, or public users.

How Information Is Used

The phone number and notification settings are used only to send requested SMS alerts to the owner's own phone number.

SMS Consent and Data Sharing

SMS opt-in consent and phone numbers are not shared, sold, rented, or transferred to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Opt-Out

The recipient may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. SMS notifications may also be disabled in the notifier application's settings.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the project owner through GitHub.
