Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Connor Knabe RuneLite Notifier, a personal RuneLite/OSRS notifier side project. The Notifier is used for low-volume SMS alerts related to the owner's own Old School RuneScape gameplay and RuneLite client notifications.

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise provide your mobile phone number, SMS opt-in data, or messaging consent information to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Purpose of SMS Messages

SMS messages are used only to send gameplay-related notifier alerts to the opted-in phone number. Alerts may include low Hitpoints, low Prayer, XP rate updates, valuable loot drops, idle status, or other configured RuneLite/OSRS notifier events.

Information Collected

The Notifier may collect and store the mobile phone number provided during optional SMS opt-in, SMS opt-in data, messaging consent information, and basic notification settings needed to send SMS alerts. The Notifier is not intended for public customer signup, lead generation, marketing, or third-party subscriber messaging.

How Information Is Used

The mobile phone number, SMS opt-in data, messaging consent information, and notification settings are used only to send requested SMS alerts related to configured RuneLite/OSRS notifier events.

SMS Message Disclosures

SMS notifications are optional. Message frequency varies based on gameplay activity and notifier settings. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

SMS Consent and Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise provide your mobile phone number, SMS opt-in data, or messaging consent information to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Opt-Out

The SMS recipient may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. The SMS recipient may reply HELP for help. SMS notifications may also be disabled in the notifier application's settings.
